The premise

Artificial intelligence is no longer limited to chatbots and self-driving cars. Startups are now using AI to analyze selfies, examine hair strands, and even adjust weights while someone is exercising.

This growing field guide follows companies applying AI to health and wellness in very different ways. Some of the technology seems genuinely useful, but none of it is perfect.
